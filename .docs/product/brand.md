# Dwellow — Brand Foundation (Screening)

> The brand for dwellow-v3, the tenant-screening product. This supersedes the older
> ticketing-era "Nothing falls through" playbook, which was written for a different
> product (two-sided maintenance-request tracking). See [product overview](./overview.md).

## 1. Positioning

**Internal positioning statement:**
Dwellow is how a small landlord chooses a tenant with confidence — a fast, structured
way to collect, compare, and judge applicants side by side, so the highest-stakes
decision in renting stops being a gut call.

**The one thing Dwellow owns: _confidence in the decision._**
Every competitor sells a _report_ (a credit pull, a background check — a document you
still have to interpret alone). Dwellow sells the _decision itself_: every applicant
scored against **your** criteria, laid out side by side, with the red flags a busy
landlord would otherwise miss surfaced for you. Not a bureau. Not a spreadsheet. The
confident answer to "who do I hand the keys to?"

## 2. Tagline

**Primary: "Know before you hand over the keys."**

Bench (test, don't default to):
- _"Choose your tenant with confidence."_ — plainest statement of the core promise
- _"Every applicant, side by side."_ — names the mechanism; good for a features section
- _"The confident way to screen."_ — shortest; good for compact placements

## 3. Personality

If Dwellow were a person: a **seasoned leasing pro who's screened 500 tenants** and
will tell you, calmly and plainly, what actually matters about this applicant — and
what doesn't. Never alarmist, never salesy, never hides behind jargon. Makes you feel
like you finally have someone in your corner for the scary part.

## 4. Voice Attributes

**Confident — not boastful**
- _We are:_ clear and sure about what the product does; we state the benefit plainly.
- _We are not:_ hypey, superlative-slinging, or making guarantees we can't stand behind.
- _Sounds like:_ "See every applicant scored against your criteria — on one screen."
- _Not:_ "The world's most powerful AI-driven tenant intelligence platform."

**Calm & reassuring — not soft**
- _We are:_ steady, anxiety-lowering; we acknowledge the stakes and then make them manageable.
- _We are not:_ mushy, vague, or avoidant of the hard parts (this is a high-consequence decision).
- _Sounds like:_ "Picking a tenant is nerve-wracking. Dwellow gives you a clear, consistent way to compare — so you're deciding on evidence, not a hunch."
- _Not:_ "Don't worry, we've got you! 🎉"

**Plain-spoken — not dumbed-down**
- _We are:_ jargon-free, concrete, short where we can be.
- _We are not:_ childish, or stripped of the substance a landlord needs to trust the tool.
- _Sounds like:_ "Applicants fill out your form and upload their documents. You never chase a pay stub over text again."

**Fair & careful — the trust pillar**
- _We are:_ honest about what the AI does (scores against _your_ rubric) and doesn't do
  (it's not a bureau report, not a legal verdict); mindful of fair-housing.
- _We are not:_ claiming to predict a "good tenant," or implying we pull regulated reports.
- _Sounds like:_ "Dwellow scores applicants on the criteria you set — income, references, rental history — not on anything it shouldn't."
- _Not:_ "Our AI knows who the bad tenants are."

**Banned words:** seamless, dive into, game-changing, leverage, unlock, revolutionary,
powerful, streamlined, effortless. Also avoid absolute predictive claims ("find the
perfect tenant," "never pick a bad tenant").

## 5. Messaging Pillars

1. **Confidence, not guesswork.** Score every applicant against _your_ criteria and
   compare them side by side. Decide on evidence.
2. **Fast and structured.** One custom form, one link, one dashboard. No email threads,
   no chasing documents, no spreadsheet.
3. **Catches what you'd miss.** A busy landlord skims. Dwellow reads every submission
   the same way, every time, and surfaces the red flags.
4. **Fair and above-board.** Documents the applicant provides — no regulated bureau
   pull, no logins for applicants, screening on criteria you control.

## 6. Audience

**Primary — DIY small landlord (1–20 units).** Self-manages between a day job and
dinner. Price-sensitive, values ease over depth, and feels real anxiety at the
screening moment. Address as a capable peer who's stretched thin — respect their time,
lower their stress, never talk down.

**Secondary — the applicant (link-only, no account).** Experiences the application
form. Brand touch here is light but matters: the form should feel **respectful, clear,
and fair** — a professional landlord, not an interrogation. This protects the
landlord's reputation and Dwellow's fair-housing posture.

## 7. Visual Direction

The screening product already leans on a **fit-score gauge** and **status-toned
evidence rows** (green = verified, amber = needs attention) — the brand should make
that the visual signature: _color does the judging work._ Calm, roomy,
evidence-forward. Trustworthy like a well-run leasing office, not clinical like a
credit bureau.
